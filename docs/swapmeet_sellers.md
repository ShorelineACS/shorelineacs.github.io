## Selling at the Swap Meet

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Table Reservations**

    ---

    Tables are a $10 donation each. Please register ahead of time by emailing [shorelineacs@gmail.com](mailto:shorelineacs@gmail.com?subject=Swap%20Meet%20Table%20Registration).

    _Table sizes and other details to come._

-   **Setup and Teardown**

    ---

    Seller setup begins at 8:30 AM. Tables must be cleared by 3:30 PM.

    _Other setup instructions to come._

-   **Accepting Payments**

    ---

    Be ready to accept cash. Checks are up to you. We also recommend accepting popular mobile payment apps such as Venmo and Zelle, since many buyers will be paying by phone.

    **Shoreline ACS cannot accept payments on behalf of private sellers.** All sales are handled directly between you and the buyer.

</div>
